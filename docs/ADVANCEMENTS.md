# Rust & Ruin advancement guide

Open **Advancements** with **L** by default and select **Rust & Ruin**. The tab unlocks automatically after you join a world. Hover an icon to read its goal. Drag or scroll to move around the tree, use Shift + scroll to move sideways, or press Home to return to the root. If Home is bound to open Advancements, use the mouse to pan back.

The tables cover the visible mod milestones. Find ingredient lists and layouts in the [crafting guide](RECIPES.md).

## Equipment, rust, and salvage

| Advancement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| **Iron Has a Darker Side** | Obtain an iron ingot. This also unlocks the mod recipes in your recipe book. |
| **Rusty Business** | Drop your Nail so it touches water or exposed rain. Let it turn into a Rusted Nail. Default times are five seconds in water or fifteen seconds in exposed rain. |
| **Keep Your Distance** | Right-click with an Iron Spear or Rusted Iron Spear to land a thrust on a target in reach. |
| **A Pointed Upgrade** | At a smithing table, leave the template slot empty, put arrows in the base slot and Rust in the addition slot, and take the Tetanized Arrows. |
| **Mind Your Fingers** | Catch something in a Bear Trap and use a Trap Reset Tool on the closed trap. |
| **Hook, Line and Tetanus** | Leave a dropped fishing rod touching water until it breaks and pick up a Rusty Fishing Hook. Each break has a 15% hook chance. Repeat with another rod if needed. |
| **Why did I do that?** | Throw a Rusted Nail upward and die to your own falling nail. |

## Disease and protection

| Advancement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| **A Painful Lesson** | Contract Tetanus from a rusty weapon, trap, or Tetanus potion. |
| **Lockjaw Sonata** | Reach the final stage of an untreated Tetanus infection or take a Lockjaw Sonata potion. |
| **A Dose of Common Sense** | Drink Tetanus Antitoxin while you have Tetanus or Lockjaw Sonata. |
| **Faucci Ouchie** | Drink either the Tetanus Vaccine or Rabies Vaccine. |
| **Hands Off** | Equip Protective Gloves in their dedicated inventory slot and let them successfully prevent an exposure from a rusty source. Protection is chance-based. |
| **Not Today, maybe tomorrow** | Hold up a shield enchanted with Tetanus Protection and block a Tetanus-inflicting hit. |
| **Strange Meat** | Obtain Infected Meat dropped by a permanently tetanized animal. |
| **Why did I eat that?** | Contract Salmonella by eating raw Infected Meat or by taking a Salmonella potion. |
| **Foaming at the Mouth** | Contract Rabies from a rabid creature, raw Rabid Meat, or a Rabies potion. |
| **Back to Yourself** | Drink Rabies Cure while you have Rabies. |

## Medics and mutations

| Advancement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| **The Doctor Is In** | Place a Medicine Cabinet. |
| **Doctor's Orders** | Complete a trade with a medic. For a treatment, write a prescription first and bring it with the requested emeralds. Supply trades also count. |
| **Special Delivery** | Sneak and right-click a medic, complete both case objectives, and return to a medic of the same kind if your case ends with fieldwork. |
| **Clean Bill of Health** | Right-click a permanently tetanized or rabid animal with a Veterinary Kit and cure it. |
| **Tetanized Mob** | Permanently convert a creature through a rusty attack or by using Rust on an iron golem. Permanent mutation uses a chance roll. |
| **Tetanized Guardian** | Permanently mutate an iron golem with a rusty attack or by using Rust on it. Each Survival attempt with Rust uses one Rust. |

## Bosses and beacons

| Advancement | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| **Tetanus. Nice One!** | Defeat a Tetanized Wither. Build the normal Wither shape using Tetanized Soul Sand, with an optional netherite block in the upper center to guarantee the special summon at default settings. |
| **A Beacon of Bad Ideas** | Place a Tetanized Beacon crafted with the Tetanized Wither Star. |
| **Rust Never Sleeps** | Build a four-layer pyramid entirely from Rusted Iron Blocks, place the Tetanized Beacon on top with clear sky, and confirm a valid power with a netherite ingot. |
| **Forged in Infection** | At the Tetanus Wither Forging Table, combine an End Crystal, a Rust, and a netherite ingot. Take the Tetanized End Crystal from the output slot. |
| **Tetanus, Again?** | After the first Ender Dragon fight, put four Tetanized End Crystals around the End exit portal. Be in the End, within 256 horizontal blocks of the portal, and out of Spectator mode at the summon. |
| **The End of Tetanus?** | Earn this when the Tetanized Dragon dies after you deal at least 20 points of damage to its health. Each player's damage is tracked separately. |

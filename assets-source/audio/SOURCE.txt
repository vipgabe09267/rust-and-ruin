Rust & Ruin audio sources

Tetanus_Effect.mp3 was supplied by the mod author and is preserved unchanged.
The existing packaged tetanus_effect.ogg is mono Vorbis, 44.1 kHz, 4.30 seconds.
Its audio is retained: the proximity fix changes positional playback and attenuation,
not the recording. Infections use at most 1.0 native gain and a 16-block attenuation
radius; the old configuration value 2.5 remains compatible but cannot expand that radius.
Vanilla clamps local mixer gain to 1.0, so this preserves the previous local loudness.

medic_work.wav is an original, deterministic, one-second mono PCM sound synthesized
by tools/Generate-Medical-Audio.ps1. It combines two short glass partial clusters,
seeded soft cloth noise and an instrument click. No external recording or sample is used.
The script writes the PCM source and encodes the packaged medic_work.ogg with FFmpeg
Vorbis quality 5, mono, 44.1 kHz, bit-exact flags and no copied metadata.

Regenerate from the repository root:
  powershell -NoProfile -File tools/Generate-Medical-Audio.ps1
Or pass -FfmpegPath to an installed FFmpeg executable. Gradle builds and players do
not need FFmpeg; the packaged OGG files are included as normal mod resources.
The medic work sound uses native profession ambience, with 12-block attenuation.

Gloves over opaque skin sleeves

Original Minecraft screenshots before and after a small clearance correction. The glove now remains visible over the player's outer skin sleeve.

What was tested

The automated development client equipped and removed the actual glove item, synchronized its server state, and captured the native Minecraft framebuffer in eight controlled views per build. A test-only skin has fully opaque blue outer sleeves and a magenta body. Each view includes an unequipped baseline.

This isolated Creative fixture used the real Night Vision status effect for visibility. Raw sky and block light remained zero in this test world; those readings, the effect acknowledgement and bright-blue baseline pixel counts are recorded in the CSVs. Night Vision did not replace the production renderer or edit the screenshots. Earlier dark fixture attempts are excluded.

The original hand and cuff surfaces sat underneath vanilla's 0.25-unit outer skin sleeve. The corrected surface clearances are 0.275 for the hand, 0.30 for the cuff and 0.15 for the thumb. Fitted dimensions, native arm widths, UVs and poses were retained.

All 16 after frames were independently inspected: complete hand and cuff are visible in each wearing view, with no obvious sleeve intersection in these views. All eight after baselines contain zero brown glove pixels; the eight equipped frames contain 3,756–22,613. Pixel counts support the visual comparison; they do not prove appearance by themselves.

Scope: vanilla opaque skin sleeves in these static views, without armor or held items. This supplement is not a claim that every skin, pose, armor combination or third-party renderer has been tested. The broader release matrix is reported separately in the main gallery.

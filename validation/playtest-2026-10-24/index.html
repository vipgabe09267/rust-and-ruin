Methods and limits

These are automated checks in an actual graphical integrated client. Crafting, smithing, potion transfers and cleaning use native client interactions; native cooking and brewing engines consume real ingredients, with long cooking waits accelerated. Ingredients and test fixtures may be prepared with Creative access. Crafted and brewed outputs are acquired rather than inserted by the test.

Advancement actions use native server gameplay methods and actual client item-use timers; synchronized native advancement widgets are inspected and captured. This does not mean every action was a physical mouse click. There are 27 visible advancement nodes and one recipe-book reward. The retired Creative-only Bad Egg ID is excluded from the 28 active advancements.

Despawn uses automatic native ticks while source chunks remain loaded and entity ticking. Six ordinary hostile variants disappear, while a name-tagged hostile and three livestock controls remain. Nine weathering receipts share three overview screenshots, labeled as shared images. These checks do not replace owner Survival playtesting or a long-running multiplayer session.

Methods and scope

The interaction run uses the actual connected client and native movement/use-key input. Combat targets are fixture-spawned with both native player-creator settings; the naturally generated camp residents are verified separately. Fixture ingredients, vaccination and deterministic mutation chances may be prepared administratively. No exposure, projectile-collision, trap-trigger or reset function was called directly to obtain these interaction results. The returning-nail cases move only the grounded player horizontally under a naturally descending shot. The actual client SoundManager records voice play events; screenshots alone cannot prove audio.

The camp was generated by the native noise terrain/chunk pipeline, then discovered by client network commands and visited. Its report records zero manual templates, starts, POIs or resident spawns. The five clinic previews intentionally place native templates by hand with entities disabled; they inspect cabinet geometry and direction. Spectator and native Night Vision are declared camera/visibility fixtures.

All published PNGs, JSON reports and logs are byte-identical to the immutable passing snapshots. The interaction client-result text was removed by a subsequent Gradle run before snapshotting; its complete PASS log and detailed native JSON receipts remain the authority. These checks do not guarantee an absence of bugs or a camp in every search area.

Traps

Survival restraint and reset from above

The actual client walked into the trap and attempted forward movement and jumping. Health became 17 HP. Across 92 server/client position samples, maximum drift from its native captured anchor was below 0.006 blocks. The real top reset then reopened the jaws, released the captive and charged one durability point.

Reset through a captive body

The trapped animal can obstruct the ordinary block raycast. The native entity interaction still reaches its trap and resets it; the second point of tool durability is charged.

Reset from the side

An elevated trap was reset by an actual side interaction, charging the third durability point.

Creative walking remains immune

Creative movement across the trap did not close it, capture the player or add injury.

Fresh mob infection, then a later repeat

The same cow first acquired a temporary infection with one client voice event. A later real projectile hit refreshed that existing infection with zero additional voice events. Mutation was disabled for this fixture.

Player infection and vaccine prevention

The client threw each nail upward. The fixture aligned only the grounded player under its naturally descending projectile; no damage or collision method was called directly. The first infection produced one voice. With vaccine protection set to 100%, the second native hit applied no Tetanus and no voice.

Camp

Native global locate and local workstation POI

The actual client sent /locate structure and the mod alias over the network; both resolved the same start at X=400, Z=160. After visiting the generated room, /locate poi found its real field cabinet at X=414, Y=-50, Z=167. Vanilla global locate intentionally displays the Y coordinate as ~.

Naturally generated residents and cabinet

This camp came from full native noise-terrain chunks in a seed-0 world with structures enabled. No template, structure start, workstation or resident was inserted manually. The client and server identified the same field medic and 200-maximum-HP tetanized guard.

Native chest loot

The generated chest retained its medic-tent loot table before opening. Native interaction then generated ten nonempty stacks, confirmed on both client and server. One discovered camp does not establish a spawn rate or guarantee a camp near every player.
